US-2b.4implemented Label a split-payment receipt (card + cash)
As a user who paid for a 50 EUR dinner: 30 EUR by card (Triodos) and 20 EUR in cash (wallet) I want add two account transactions to the same receipt (one for the card, one for cash) so that the 30 EUR card portion is matched to my bank CSV and the 20 EUR cash portion is recorded as a wallet expense.
